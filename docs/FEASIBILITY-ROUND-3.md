## What the numbers mean

No complete site-level paid-demand series, scoped new-project cost package or firm expansion-capacity commitment was obtained. Historical equipment, tariffs, grant records, advertised prices and utility procedures have narrower scopes. The 111 main stress scenarios and 18 previous conditional tariff subtotals were recomputed and remain numerically unchanged from v0.3.2. Two additional Medford Schedule 30/201 subtotal cases include known riders and an explicitly inferred public-purpose charge mapping; the 1200kW conservative case is outside that schedule and is left unpriced. This is not proof that any actual parcel must lose money.

The conservative stress case assumes 8 stalls × 2 sessions/day ×22kWh ×365 ×$0.40 = $51,392 annual gross revenue, below its $60,000 assumed non-utility fixed costs before electricity, service or replacement. Every property therefore fails that shared stress setup mechanically. The scenario is not an observed local downside forecast.

Both lenses still require base payback ≤6 years, conservative payback ≤8 years from first spend, base NPV ≥0 at 10% real across10 operating years, positive conservative steady cash, and all six evidence gates. These are chosen underwriting rules, not Tesla standards.

## Acquisition standard and stopping conditions

- Demand: obtain anonymized paid sessions, delivered kWh, realized price net of discounts, refunds and tax, timestamps, uptime and outages for at least 12 consecutive months where available. Separate free sessions and record coverage gaps. Existing-station performance is an observation; an incremental new-station forecast additionally needs displacement, capture, ramp and competition assumptions. No customer identities are needed.
- Complete cost: reconcile EVSE, power cabinet, civil works, trenching, drainage, access, transformer/switchgear, utility contributions, easements, design/permits, contingency, lease, insurance, payment/software, maintenance and replacements. Match all quotes to one layout, load design and cost boundary; reconcile included supplier services before adding allowances. Historical grant awards or building-permit valuations are not all-in project budgets.
- Connection: obtain owner-authorized service identity, existing 15-minute demand, firm spare capacity, connection design, applicable tariff/riders/taxes, utility contribution, equipment procurement and energization schedule. Charger output nameplate and existing generation are not spare grid import. Battery buffering changes both power and lifecycle costs.
- Site rights, permits/access and commercial terms remain separate mandatory gates. A suitable technical design does not supply a lease, exclusivity waiver or authority to resell electricity.
- No owner/operator contact, application, records submission, paid study or contract was performed. The routes above are proposed next steps. Failed access and nonpublic data are not fabricated as zero.

## Inverse calculation limits

The app and CSV provide required sessions/energy and maximum capex, all-in purchased-electricity price, monthly fixed/holding envelope and construction duration. Each changes only one input. Base tests include NPV; conservative tests include positive steady cash. These independent boundaries cannot be combined into a jointly feasible case. All money is constant 2026 USD.

Required volumes are unconstrained algebra, not forecasts or capacity-approved targets. Average grid input = required delivered kWh per day ÷24 ÷(1−loss), which is not a peak-service rating. Very large thresholds may be physically impossible. A nonpositive contribution margin has no finite volume solution; a “no solution” cost/delay limit means even reducing that single input to zero cannot pass with the other assumptions fixed. It does not establish parcel impossibility.
