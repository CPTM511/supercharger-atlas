## Qualification remains separate

Both perspectives retain the chosen policy: base payback at most six years and conservative payback at most eight years from first spend; nonnegative base NPV at a 10% real discount rate over ten operating years; positive conservative cash after replacement allowance; and verified site rights, grid/tariff, complete cost, paid demand/pricing, permits/access and commercial terms. These are underwriting choices, not Tesla policy or industry standards. Expansion and recovery require incremental cash flows. No target count overrides a gate.

Challis ranks first for network investigation because of a retained corridor gap, while ranking tenth for third-party investigation because demand and delivery economics remain weakly evidenced. Machias has a consequential funding-eligibility question, not an award. No favorable isolated scenario is a joint gate pass.
