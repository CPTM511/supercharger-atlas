# Round-one public-evidence review — October 30, 2026

**Outcome: zero qualified properties under either lens.** All 20 former shortlist locations and three replacement prospects remain pending. Fourteen reserves were carried forward without a new original-source review. The application separately calculates stress scenarios for all 37 properties.

The round checked public property identities, the strongest supply/cancellation/permit risks and what evidence exists for site control, utility capacity, engineering quotes, permits and measured demand. Category checks are not site visits or independent measurements. The per-property ledger distinguishes verified public records, unresolved conflicts, unknowns and inaccessible sources.

## Findings that matter

- **Mansfield:** the original city report, PDF page 4, lists a charging project at 1715 E Broad as approved/under construction. The November 2023 issued-permit report also lists CR-22-05845 for charging in an existing parking lot with new Oncor service, and BE-23-06019. The H-E-B candidate is at **1670**, not 1715. Neither report proves current operation, power or public access. [Quarterly report](https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/15687/Mansfield-Quarterly-Q1-24-comp), [issued permits](https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/422).
- **Machias:** the project at Hannaford is marked canceled in the official program list. Multiple projects in its batch were canceled; the reason is unknown. Cancellation is neither proof of parcel failure nor permission to reuse another developer's agreement. [Efficiency Maine](https://www.efficiencymaine.com/at-work/electric-vehicle-supply-equipment-initiative/).
- **Hanksville:** the operator expressly describes RV-outlet charging without dedicated EV chargers, restricted to unreserved RV spaces and specified hours. The existing service does not establish DCFC infrastructure or sales. [Operator information](https://www.dukesslickrockcampground.com/ev-camping).
- **Austin:** public evidence describes existing charging, and historical utility material records upgrade discussions. This remains an upgrade/co-location investigation; there is no demonstrated empty greenfield gap. See the candidate's linked tourism and utility records.
- **Candidate replacements:** Cross Creek H-E-B, WinCo Spokane Valley and WinCo Powell were reviewed as alternatives. Existing/planned supply, unresolved demand and missing execution evidence prevent promotion. Access failures and an unsubmitted/default permit-search screen are logged; neither was interpreted as absence of permits.

## What remains unknown

No reviewed property has an enforceable site-control agreement, utility capacity/connection commitment, scoped all-in project quote, defensible measured site sales or complete supplier/commercial terms. No property receives a qualified rank. The scenario hurdle also fails for every reference input set, independently of the evidence failures; this does not establish actual parcel losses.

There was no owner outreach, utility application, site visit, exhaustive title/permit search or private customer-data collection. The next decisive work requires authorized access to these missing documents and measurements, not another layer of generic public-market estimates.
