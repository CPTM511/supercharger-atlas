## Energy and service constraints

59 sessions and 35 billed kWh imply 2,065 billed kWh/day. Grid energy assumes 15% losses; this is not a measured meter-to-billing reconciliation. A permanent 150 kW site import cap and targeted summer 4CP curtailment are different designs. Daily energy averages cannot establish 15-minute peaks, queues or access rights.

59 × 35 = **2065 billed kWh/day**; at the assumed 15% loss, **2429.41 grid kWh/day**. Four posts imply **14.75 sessions/post/day** and **516.25 billed kWh/post/day**.

A continuous 150 kW grid cap requires **16.20 equivalent full-power hours/day**. Over 24 hours the energy-average load is 101.23 kW; over a 17-hour store window it is 142.91 kW, or **95.27%** of that cap's theoretical energy capacity. With four cars sharing it equally, only 31.875 billed kW/car remains: at least **65.9 minutes** for 35 kWh before taper or overhead. This is a necessary energy bound, not a queue simulation or a customer acceptance forecast.
