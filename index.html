02 / WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE DECISION

Promising sites still need commercial proof

Investment readiness remains unproven. Paid demand at a viable price, complete project costs and binding delivery terms must work together; favorable assumptions alone do not qualify a site.

Ronald Reagan analysis ↗
01

Paid demand

Obtain nearby settled transactions at comparable prices, billed energy, uptime and queues. Test whether volume transfers to this exact site.

02

Complete cost & site rights

Secure parking/access terms and a design-matched EPC, utility and operating-cost package. Expansion or recovery needs incremental cash flows.

03

Grid & peak control

Confirm meter, tariff, firm capacity/date and a priced Tesla/PEC control plan. Test lost sales and missed peaks; a 150 kW 4CP target is not a permanent power cap.

One standard for both perspectives

Payback from first spend: base ≤ 6 years; conservative ≤ 8 years. Base NPV ≥ 0 at 10% real over 10 operating years; positive conservative cash after replacement allowance; all six evidence gates verified. These are chosen underwriting rules, not Tesla policy.

Unawarded incentives remain sensitivities. Grants, rebates and credits only improve a qualifying case when eligibility, timing and costs are substantiated. Incentive conditions ↗
